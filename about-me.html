About Me

I'm a science educator completing my M.Ed. in Secondary Education at DePaul University, where I work as an AUSL Resident Teacher through AmeriCorps. Right now I teach five sections of high school Chemistry at George Washington High School in Chicago, including IB MYP courses.

Before transitioning into teaching, I spent several years as a research chemist, synthesizing and characterizing peptides and RNA conjugates at biotech companies in Boston and Madison. That background in the lab shapes how I teach: I want students to see science as something real, precise, and genuinely interesting rather than a set of formulas to memorize.

Molecute started as a small collection of HTML simulations I built to scaffold abstract concepts for my students. It's grown into a full suite of interactive tools covering atomic structure, bonding, stoichiometry, and more, all designed to meet students where they are and make the invisible visible. When I'm not building simulations, I'm probably sewing, reading, or chasing my toddler around a park where we live on the north side of Chicago.
